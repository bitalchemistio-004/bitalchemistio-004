## About me

- I build **MBUDDY**, an AI companion with three bodies: a PC app, an Android app and an ESP32 desk device that share one mind
- **AI and agents:** local LLMs (Ollama), cloud models, tool use, self-checking work, a learning memory in Obsidian
- **Voice:** wake word, speech in (Whisper) and out (Kokoro), on the GPU
- **Stack:** Python, Kotlin/Android, Rust + Tauri, JavaScript, C++ on ESP32
- **Principles:** only real features, privacy by default, the user decides

### MBUDDY
**One buddy. Three bodies. Built in the open.**

An assistant that listens, talks back, remembers, plans your day and does real work inside your apps, on your
own hardware or a cloud brain you choose.

**What it does**
- Talks and listens with a "hey buddy" wake word
- Plans your day, reminders, focus sprints with distractions off
- Opens, focuses and closes apps, controls music, always asking first
- Works inside apps and checks its own result
- Learns from what worked and what didn't

**Tech:** `Python` `Kotlin` `Tauri` `ESP32` `Ollama` `Whisper` `Kokoro`
